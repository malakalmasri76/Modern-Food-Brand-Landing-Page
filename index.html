Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Add the fruit you want to your cart, then press "Order via WhatsApp". Your order is written for you, so you only need to send it.

How fast is delivery?

Orders are picked and packed on the same day and delivered within 24 hours, depending on your area.

Can I order for an office or a café?

Yes. Bulk and weekly orders are welcome. Mention the quantities in your message and we will confirm the price.

What if the fruit is not fresh?

Send us a photo within a few hours of delivery and we will replace the item or refund it.

How do I pay?

Payment is arranged when we confirm your order on WhatsApp, usually on delivery.
